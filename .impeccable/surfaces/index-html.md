Scope: `index.html`, the single public personal page. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience, job, action and constraints unchanged from PRODUCT.md: recruiters screening for Senior
QA / SDET and backend roles, mostly mid-triage, often on a phone, who must leave with
`Manuel_Frana_CV.pdf`. Email / LinkedIn / GitHub only — no phone, no location. No repo links until
the repos are confirmed public. Catox is live but private: no playable link, no player counts.

**History.** Build 1 was a light engineering-drawing sheet with Catox leading full-bleed; rejected.
Build 2 replaced it with this dark world. The user then removed the nixie tubes entirely
("replace the tubes with simple numbers, I do not like the tubes at all") and removed the Courts
code snippet, supplying their own framing for what Courts is for. Those instructions override the
contract below wherever they conflict.

User decisions now in force: dark background, "more modern"; first viewport is **name, role and
the CV button and nothing else**; **no slogan or tagline anywhere**; Catox and Courts at the very
bottom as the payoff for reaching the end; **no nixie tubes** — figures are plain numerals; **no
code block in Courts**; Courts leads with why it exists, in the user's own words.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The page is a blackened instrument chassis that stays dark, quiet and unadorned the
whole way down, and spends its one warm colour only where something is actually being reported.
It refuses the stat-wall this world tempts: figures live inside sentences, and the only two
standalone numerals on the page sit in the last third, beside the projects that earned them.

**OWN-WORLD:** Panel ink `#08090B` ground, chassis black `#0E0E10`, gunmetal `#1C1F24` panels,
mesh `#3A3D42` and steel `#5C6168` for rules and screws only, label grey `#7C838C` for every
engraved caps label, plasma orange `#FF8A00` as the single warm signal. Big Shoulders Display in
caps for chassis lettering and for the two standalone figures, Chivo for prose, Azeret Mono for
every label and code line. Components are instrument furniture: engraved plates with corner
screws, hairline-bordered gunmetal panels, a fixed chassis rail. No rounded cards, no blur, no
glow. Orange is signal, never decoration.

**STORY:** The visitor meets a quiet, serious dark instrument, learns what he does from plain
panels, reaches two personal projects at the end that show he builds and ships things on his own
time, and leaves with the CV.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Panel-ink ground, near-empty, the hero owning the full viewport at every
width. His name in large chassis caps, the role beneath in tracked mono, and the CV button as an
engraved plate with corner screws. Nothing else, no tagline, nothing glowing.

**FORM:** Nixie-tube laboratory counter (`signals-instruments-nixie-laboratory-counter`), locked
by the user over the assigned roll (candidate 7, terminal multiplexer) and over IMPECCABLE'S PICK
(observability console). Seed key `fb86dde3`. **The world's literal carrier — the tube — was
removed by the user; what survives is its chassis: the palette, the engraved-plate components,
the caps lettering and the single emitted-signal colour.**

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
